TrainingSamples (data collection)

Final flow
Collection mode: ESP32 → POST /api/training-samples (with label) → TrainingSamples
Export to CSV, train in ML.NET offline, get model.zip
Put model.zip in the API. The ESP32 then posts to POST /api/readings (no label) → the backend predicts → saves to Readings
React → GET /api/readings/latest every second → shows PredictedLabel
